# Mobile hero and footer refinement

## Scope
- Preserve the current Crescent & Pearl hero styling, headline, buttons, colors, and animation language.
- Replace only the tooth artwork with a friendly human dentist character on a transparent background, retaining the existing gentle float.
- Reframe and constrain the hero on phones so the image sits close to the copy without creating a large blank area.
- Shorten the 24-word emergency warning into one compact, easy-to-scan line while retaining the urgent booking link and hospital guidance.
- Improve the lower homepage on mobile: make dentist discovery easier to scan, keep the query guide clearly marked as an unsaved demo, and prevent the floating guide from covering content.
- Redesign the footer for mobile as a compact, polished finish with essential patient links first, clinic details grouped clearly, and staff-demo links de-emphasized.

## Visual direction
- Keep the existing warm cream, teal, and gold identity.
- Use Narowal Dental Clinic only for inspiration from its clear booking hierarchy, clinic credibility, and practical contact layout.
- Do not copy its identity, dentist details, patient counts, credentials, reviews, photos, or claims.
- Keep real photography in supporting sections; the hero remains a soft illustrated experience.

## Mobile checks
- Verify the first screen at 390px shows the warning, navigation, headline, primary action, and a meaningfully framed part of the dentist character.
- Verify there is no oversized blank gap in the hero.
- Verify the homepage ending and footer have no overlap, clipping, or floating-button obstruction.
- Confirm desktop presentation remains visually unchanged apart from the requested character replacement and shorter warning.
